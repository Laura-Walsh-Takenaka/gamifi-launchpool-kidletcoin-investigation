### Settlement and technical distinctions that materially change the next reconstruction

**Shibafriend and FOMO are different settlement problems.** GamiFi's 29 June Shibafriend cancellation notice offered participants either a private allocation with 1,000 GMI or a refund after changed tokenomics/release timing. The preserved later frontend still labels that project cancelled. These establish representations and a campaign identifier, not choices made or payment completion. [W1, A6]

FOMO's own September 27 release directed legacy BSC holders to `deposit.fomolab.app` with September 29 claims, and seed/private holders to `dashboard.fomolab.app`. The original GamiFi IDO, relaunched IDO, legacy-token migration and seed/private allocations require separate opening balances and settlement evidence before combining any totals. [W2–W4, A1–A3]

**Returning a participation stake is not refunding an IDO purchase.** In the recovered current `Project.sol`, `Funding` records the project ID, paid amount and token allocation; a separate allocation-rate setter can alter the executable ratio. The `ClaimBack` route returns the GMI participation stake, while project funding uses the payment token. A `ClaimBack` event therefore must not be counted automatically as a BUSD refund. This is a source-level guide; historical deployed implementations and individual receipt assets still control. [G1]

**Paid access has at least three events to reconcile: payment, activation and use.** Current repository `MemberCard.sol` initializes payment in BUSD, a 100-BUSD fee, and treasury `0x35b119730F79881DAc623dc51c831C6A04cAB5f3`. Its `buy` transfers payment directly from purchaser to treasury and mints the NFT; separate admin activation sets membership dates/counter. Admin mints can set membership directly and do not establish payment. Treasury and membership settings are mutable. Thus a complete paid-sales ledger needs BUSD transfers and invocation methods; delivery needs activation, expiry/extension and counter/vendor usage. The initial treasury is a source-role link to the prior ID4 withdrawal recipient, not proof who controlled it or that every deployment used that initial value. [G2]

Replaying one contract's present supply cannot cover both periods. The full prior address-version ledger remains the reference for exact page dates.

**Staking principal and promised rewards require separate accounting.** In repository `StakingV3.sol`, the same-token owner withdrawal takes balance minus `_stakedAmount`. That is an excess-over-recorded-principal calculation; it does not subtract accrued rewards. The source also pays pending rewards before returning principal during unstaking, so insufficient reward liquidity can cause that transaction to revert before principal repayment; this is a code-path inference, not a finding that a particular user actually failed to withdraw.
