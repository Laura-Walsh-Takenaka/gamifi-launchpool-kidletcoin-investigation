# Rights and sources

This repository combines research text, analytical derivatives, public records, technical data and references to third-party publications. Those categories do not necessarily share the same ownership or reuse terms.

## Forking and reuse

The project invites viewing, forking, verification and contributions through GitHub, subject to GitHub's terms and the rights that apply to each item. **No broader repository-wide content license is asserted in this edition.** The invitation does not grant unrestricted rights to republish, commercialize or sublicense third-party material.

No Creative Commons or software license is applied to the whole collection. Existing licenses accompanying particular source material remain applicable to that material. Newly submitted work should identify its authoring and reuse basis without claiming rights over underlying third-party sources.

GitHub's [repository licensing guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository) explains the distinction between public viewing/forking and broader licensed reuse. GitHub also maintains a [copyright takedown policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/dmca-takedown-policy).

## Source attribution

Public-source URLs, original publication titles, publisher names, transaction hashes, company identifiers and archive capture dates should remain attached to the claims they support. A third-party statement is attributed to its publisher; inclusion does not imply the publisher endorses this research.

Source records and derivative research serve different purposes. Where a PDF or exhibit has been edited, annotated or redacted, use its public source locator to consult the underlying record. Do not treat presentation changes as authentication of the source's claims.

An inventory or manifest identifies the delivered files. A URL or source-register entry may point to a record that is not redistributed here. A checksum confirms the identity of particular bytes; it does not establish authorship, accuracy, legal status or the truth of an allegation.

## Historical scope

Research observations are principally from 5–7 September 2026, with their individual dates preserved. The repository packaging date is 20 September 2026. Later changes in source pages, balances, corporate status or record-access procedures require new checks.

Contemporaneous primary records and reproducible observations should be assessed before analytical summaries. Later explicit corrections qualify earlier text. Same-date reports may contain different stages of work: consult the topic-specific continuation and correction notes rather than assuming the date alone settles precedence.

## Corrections and rights concerns

Use a **Correction or rebuttal** issue to identify a disputed claim, attribution error, outdated interpretation or rights concern. Specify the repository path and enough context to locate the material. Do not post identity documents or additional private information to support the request.

For claims about historical events, provide the best available public supporting source and explain how it changes the assessment. For a redistribution concern, identify the material and the basis of the concern so the relevant item can be reviewed.
