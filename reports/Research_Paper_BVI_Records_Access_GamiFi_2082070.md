1. Conclusion

The exact search target is Gamifi Limited, BVI company 2082070. Its official filing history and
historical ownership remain unobtained. Selected older Gazette notices are freely accessible, so
a paid subscription is not the only route. [BASE-01; R1; GZ-04-GZ-05]

November 2021 comes only from a secondary directory. No official certificate establishes that date, current
status, agent, directors or members. The screenshot does not show a fee; official sources separately

3. What BVI products provide

The ordinary report is a corporate filing index. Its transaction history means filings with the Registry, not
bank or token transactions. Copies, inspection, current director names and restricted registers are separate
products. Current ownership alone would not identify a person who controlled a wallet in 2022. [R1; R6-R9]

GamiFi's compliance from a generic deadline. It is not a promised 2021-2022 ledger. Directors and members
also have specified inspection rights under section 100; GMI token ownership alone does not establish
membership. [R4; R9-R11; R16]

Restricted beneficial-ownership access
The FSC announced launch on 1 April 2026, following its 30 March circular. The process requires a
qualifying legitimate interest, identification, contact information, entity name, signed purpose statement and
limited-use declaration. The Registrar assesses eligibility and objections/exemptions apply. Disclosure
concerns natural persons meeting 25% ownership/voting or specified control tests, not every shareholder.
Company, agent and insolvent-liquidator requests have a separate route under regulation 31B(2A).
[BO-01-BO-03; BO-05]

Reviewed guidance specifies US$75 non-refundable per application, plus US$75 certification if
requested. S.I.119/2025 and S.I.120/2025 tie the relevant commencement to 29 December 2025. The
circular describes English PDF supporting evidence, certified valid KYC documents, registered-agent notice
and a 12-day operational timetable without objection or opposition; a disputed application is not guaranteed
within that period. No free substitute, applicable waiver, requester entitlement or GamiFi BO filing was
established. The linked login destination could not be opened by the automated reader. [BO-01-BO-06]
