1. Conclusion

The UK company's initial shareholders, Carajo's Panama formation and its 2025 appointments
are documented. The historical website's offshore company, ultimate ownership and transaction
authority remain unresolved. Laura's dated Launchpool COO and GamiFi CEO roles are supported by
contemporary publications. They do not identify her as an offshore shareholder, wallet controller or recipient
of proceeds.

The two Panama deeds have been received and reviewed. They should no longer appear on
missing-document lists. Their missing companions are the issued-share register, voting-shareholder records,
formation instructions and executed business-transfer agreements.
