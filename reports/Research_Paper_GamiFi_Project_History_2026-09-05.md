 GamiFi.gg / GMI
 Project history, law and blockchain evidence


  Expanded archive investigation and verified contract analysis


  5 September 2026 | Public records, historical archives and selected chain checks

  The strongest documented legal inquiry is misleading promotion. A paid NFT offer advertised
  access to three IDOs after the launchpad announced a pause. The archive also preserves
   contradictory NFT quantities, a tenfold token-price correction and a valuation error. These are specific
  representations to assess for materiality and responsibility; they are not proof of fabricated trading
  volume.

   Parts of the product existed. The chain now verifies 200 Mystery Box NFTs, 193 Duke character
  NFTs and two membership contracts with supplies of 12 and 100. NFT supply is not a paid-sales count.
  The Mystery Box and Duke implementations were independently recompiled and matched every byte
   of live code.

  The advertised burn has a real mechanism, with an important distinction. Public purchases
  send 25,000 GMI from the buyer and an equal prefunded contribution to the designated sink at
  current settings. Owner batch issuance sends only the prefunded contribution. That sink holds
  9,850,042 GMI; this balance does not identify every contributing transaction. A read-only purchase
  simulation currently fails because the contract has no matching reserve.

  The checked markets have very little quote liquidity. Three factory-verified PancakeSwap pools
  hold approximately 0.8442 WBNB, 0.00468 BUSD and 0.02076 USDT respectively. Thin present
   liquidity establishes neither who removed earlier funds nor a wash-trading scheme. A complete
  24-block check around the announced listing minute found no GMI/pool logs; this is a bounded
   historical result, not a full trading audit.

   Laura's CEO and promotional role is documented. Her own dated statements matter
  independently of wallet custody. GamiFi announced a successor on 1 September 2022; the October
  pause and later stale offers must not automatically be assigned to her. The current shared
   contract-control address has no established human identity.

  The legal analysis separates offences, evidence and dates. Canadian misleading-advertising,
  fraud and securities provisions have different requirements. Archived terms claim a BVI issuer and
  exclude Canada; actual territorial connections remain material. BVI investment law requires
   product/activity classification, and the VASP regime began in February 2023. No reviewed record
   establishes a GamiFi conviction or proves Laura controlled manipulative trading.

  The following pages retain the full project chronology and document changes, then explain the new
  on-chain results and applicable legal tests. Original records, source code, compiler outputs, RPC
  responses and primary legal sources are preserved in the accompanying ZIP.

 3A. Further changes in offering terms

   specifies $150,000 at $0.22 per token, approximately 681,818 tokens. The corrected version specifies
  $0.022 and approximately 6.818 million tokens for the same target. Its metadata dates the edit to
  March 5; the first recovered corrected capture is August 10. This establishes conflicting published
   unit-price terms and the document's revision metadata. It does not establish which price buyers
   actually paid. Original capture; first recovered corrected capture.

  May staking: larger published maximum amounts. Both May 4 pool announcements initially list
  1.5 million GMI as the maximum staking amount for each public pool. Later versions list 6 million for
  the 60-day pool and 3.4 million for the 90-day pool, while retaining the 150% and 175% APY labels.
  Both articles' metadata dates the edits to May 7; the first recovered changed bodies are June 27. A
  published limit is not a measured deposit or reward reserve. Original pool-open notice; revised notice.

  FOMO: the same announcement carried two schedules. The initial May 5 article schedules
   whitelisting May 6–9, funding May 9–13 and listing May 16. The later version changes these to May
  9–13, May 16–23 and May 30. The separate May 23 notice then delays the token-generation event to
  Q3 and promises refunds. September's later launch/distribution report does not settle whether those
  May refunds occurred. Initial May article.

  Why these distinctions matter: there are several kinds of problems in the record: arithmetic
  mistakes, revised commercial limits, rescheduled offerings, contradictory terms within a single
  document, and an offer left online after a service pause. Each has different implications. A numerical
   correction is not automatically a false sale receipt; a changed schedule is not automatically a failed
  refund; an unremoved buy page is not proof of a later completed sale.

   later captures, dated February 2024 through November 2025, match earlier versions of their 25

  7. Misleading promotion and criminal fraud

  My assessment: materially misleading promotion is the strongest documentary legal
   inquiry. The most direct comparison is the October 2023 offer of 100 BUSD for
  three-IDOs/three-months access against the October 2022 IDO pause. The 1,000/500 quantity conflict,
   tenfold price correction and valuation discrepancy are additional representations. Materiality,
  surrounding disclosures, publication responsibility and territorial connection must be assessed
  separately for each.

   Civil misleading advertising  Competition Act 74.01(1)(a). A materially false or misleading
   public representation promoting a product or business interest can be reviewable conduct. This limb
  does not require knowledge or recklessness. General impression and literal meaning both matter.
  Section 74.03(4) does not require proof an individual was deceived or that a member of the audience
  was in Canada. Consequently, a completed NFT purchase is not a prerequisite to this route. 2022
   provision; interpretation.

   Criminal misleading advertising  Competition Act 52. Knowledge or recklessness is additional
   to materially misleading public promotion; permitting a representation is included. Again, actual
  deception is not indispensable. Repeated publication after a person learned an offer was false would
  be relevant evidence; a stale page alone does not establish that knowledge. The public pause and
   disclosed corrections must be included in the context. Laura's September 2022 succession prevents
  automatically assigning her later publication failures. 2022 criminal provision.

 7B. The BVI issuer claim and the applicable dates

  Archived terms claim GamiFi Limited was incorporated in the British Virgin Islands. That is a
  recovered company representation, not an independently verified corporate certificate. Governing-law
  wording does not by itself settle regulatory jurisdiction or classification. The analysis below applies
   conditionally to the identified issuer and activities. Archived terms.

  The 2022 investment-law question is about rights and business activity. The FSC's July 2020
  guidance distinguishes pure payment/access utility from products carrying additional investment
   rights. A token's existence or staking APY alone does not establish an investment under SIBA. The
   recurring partner-offering business and percentage fee deserve closer classification than minting the
   issuer's own NFT in isolation. FSC 2020 guidance.

  SIBA licensing and deceptive inducements are separate routes. Section 4 addresses
  investment-business licensing, including its rule for BVI companies' overseas investment business.
  Schedules 1 and 2 define investments/activities and specified own-issuance exclusions. Section 91
  concerns knowing/reckless investment inducements and intentional false market impressions, subject
   to its own in/from-BVI requirement and section 92 defences. Do not substitute incorporation alone for
   that territorial test. Section 19's misleading-advertising rule applies to licensees. The reviewed
   consolidation expressly excludes Part II from commencement; this report therefore does not found a
  2022 prospectus accusation on that printed but excluded text. SIBA consolidation.

  The VASP regime started on 1 February 2023. Existing in-scope businesses had until 31 July
  2023 to apply; timely applicants could continue pending determination. It cannot be applied
   retroactively to create a 2022 VASP-registration offence. FSC commencement notice.

  For later activity, the service definition matters. The VASP Act covers specified services
  conducted as a business for another person, including certain issuer-sale financial services, exchange
  and custody. NFT/DeFi terminology is not an automatic exemption or inclusion. Section 5 includes
  relevant holding-out by BVI companies; section 24 addresses knowing/reckless material
  misstatements by a VASP. An obsolete own-NFT page alone does not establish that an operator
  continued a regulated third-party launchpad. Senior-officer liability under section 46(2) requires
  knowing authorization, permission or acquiescence. Laura's replacement as CEO in September 2022
  cannot automatically establish her conduct in 2023. VASP Act; registration guidance.

   entries including repeated categories/representatives, returned no Gami match. This is neither a
   historical no-application certificate nor proof of unlawful operation. No relevant FSC enforcement
   notice was recovered in the stated search. Current register.

  substance of the products, and actual regulated activity on the relevant dates. Token creation, high
   returns, stale sites and thin liquidity do not independently supply every element of a criminal trading
