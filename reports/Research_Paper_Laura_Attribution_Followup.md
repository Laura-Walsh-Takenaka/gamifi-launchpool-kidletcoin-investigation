Present conclusion

The November 2022 permission changes, removal of 44 million GMI and sale for
5,174.293394005109506048 BUSD remain documented. Laura's instruction, historical signing-key
custody, personal receipt and the cause of her departure remain unproved. Her public role and a
short farewell cannot supply those links; succession also does not prove that every key was surrendered.

The earlier 63,001.817377359365034843 BUSD campaign subtotal now has materially different outcomes:
original FOMO principal was fully refunded, original GAMI allocations match every funding
address and are backed, and Time Raiders' BSC cash balance is located. The August treasury sweep
and project 4 circuit identify sharper accounting questions. They do not establish a stolen total. [R4-R6]

Departure and separate technical handovers

An official 27 August 2022 post still described Laura as CEO, while promoting an interview published 2
August. Eleanor Rooney was announced 1 September. The contemporaneous Medium/Wayback
announcement gives no departure reason, misconduct allegation or handover terms. This fixes the public
presentation change within five days, not the internal notice period or whether she was dismissed. Eleanor's
3 October operational pause cites token performance, rebuilding and market conditions; it does not explain
Laura's departure. [S1-S4]
